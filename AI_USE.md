# AI use and disclosure

## Use in this project

GitHub Copilot assistance was used during development, including AI-assisted
or AI-generated code, documentation and explanations. The assistance included
project planning; workbook inspection and data-quality investigation;
preparation and preprocessing implementation; model evaluation and SHAP
reporting; API, frontend and Docker implementation; test generation; and
documentation drafting and revision. The researcher provided project
requirements, made or approved research decisions and requested review of
intermediate findings.

ChatGPT was identified as a possible supporting tool, but this disclosure does
not assert that it was used for specific work without a researcher-confirmed
record. For the final submission, record additional tools, dates, purposes and
affected work as required by course and institutional policies.

## Review and validation

AI suggestions are not authoritative research evidence. The researcher and
project contributors are responsible for reviewing AI-assisted changes, checking
official FinAccess documentation, approving methodological decisions and
verifying that the code and results are appropriate and reproducible. The
project's recorded validation includes automated Python and frontend tests,
model and data provenance checks, and synthetic API and Docker checks; these
tests do not replace human review or independently validate survey methodology.
The [README](README.md) and [evaluation report](reports/results/model_evaluation_report.md)
record the results and their limitations.

In particular, the provider's target derivation and survey-design crosswalk
remain unverified, the previously inspected test partition is not a pristine
independent assessment, and SHAP outputs are approximate model associations,
not causal explanations. AI must not invent target definitions, variable
meanings, survey procedures, citations, experiments or results.

## Privacy and accountability

Do not upload the raw FinAccess workbook, respondent records, the fitted model
with support vectors, credentials, personal information or other restricted
material to an AI service. Use only tools and data handling allowed by the
dataset terms and institutional policy. Retain prompts or outputs only when
those policies permit it.

Disclose material AI assistance in the final submission as required. Human
contributors remain accountable for the accuracy, privacy, integrity and
appropriate use of the final work, regardless of AI involvement.
