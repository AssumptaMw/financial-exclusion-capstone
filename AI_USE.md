# AI use and disclosure

AI tools, including GitHub Copilot and ChatGPT, may be used as assistants for project planning, drafting documentation, suggesting code, explaining errors, and proposing tests.

AI-generated suggestions are not authoritative research evidence. Project contributors remain responsible for reviewing and testing code, validating statistical and domain assumptions, checking all factual claims against official FinAccess and course sources, and ensuring that results can be reproduced. AI tools must not be used to invent target definitions, variable meanings, survey procedures, citations, or results. AI-generated explanations must not be presented as causal findings.

Do not upload the raw FinAccess workbook, credentials, personal information, or other restricted material to an AI service. Use only tools and data handling permitted by the dataset terms and institutional policy.

Disclose material AI assistance in the final submission according to course and institutional requirements. Where required, record the tool, purpose, date, and the portions of work affected; retain prompts or outputs only when permitted by those policies. Human review and verification remain required regardless of disclosure.
